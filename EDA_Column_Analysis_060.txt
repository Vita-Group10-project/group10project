Open Payments Product-Level Fields 
1. Overview

This section of the CMS Open Payments dataset captures product information associated with manufacturer payments.
Each payment record may reference up to five products, represented by repeating field groups (_1 through _5).

These fields support product-level transparency, compliance review, and spending analysis across drugs, biologicals, devices, and medical supplies.

2. Covered_or_Noncovered_Indicator (1–5)
Description

Indicates whether the referenced product is considered a Covered Product under federal healthcare program rules.

Business Meaning
Indicator	Definition
Covered	Product typically requires a prescription or FDA approval and is eligible for reimbursement by federal programs (e.g., Medicare, Medicaid).
Non-Covered	Product does not meet reimbursement criteria; often over-the-counter or not federally reimbursed.
Examples
Indicator	Example Products
Covered	Cancer drugs, insulin, heart stents
Non-Covered	OTC vitamins, tongue depressors
Analytical Importance

Supports regulatory and compliance analysis

Enables comparison of covered vs non-covered promotional spending

Identifies higher-risk payment associations

3. Indicate_Drug_or_Biological_or_Device_or_Medical_Supply (1–5)
Description

Identifies the type of product associated with the payment.

Observed Distribution (Sample)
Product Type	Record Count
Drug	8,527,892
Device	3,167,252
Biological	2,393,512
Medical Supply	86,715
Null	1,222,253
Invalid values (e.g., “Covered”, “Yes”)	Minimal
Observations

Drugs represent the majority of product references.

Null values indicate payments not tied to a specific product.

Invalid categorical values reflect data quality issues.

4. Product_Category_or_Therapeutic_Area (1–5)
Description

Classifies the product by clinical area or functional category.

Usage

Drugs & Biologicals → Therapeutic Area
(e.g., Oncology, Cardiology, Endocrinology)

Devices → Product Category
(e.g., Orthopedics, Surgical Equipment, Cardiac Stents)

Key Characteristics

Free-text values

Mixed upper/lowercase

Inconsistent naming conventions

Analytical Value

Enables spending analysis by therapeutic area

Supports company and product strategy comparisons

Helps identify concentration of payments in specific clinical domains

5. Name_of_Drug_or_Biological_or_Device_or_Medical_Supply (1–5)
Description

The specific product name associated with the payment.

Importance

Most human-readable and searchable product field

Enables drug-level and device-level tracking

More precise than category-level analysis

Use Cases

Identify top-promoted drugs or devices

Analyze manufacturer spending by individual product

Track promotion trends over time

6. Associated_Drug_or_Biological_NDC (1–5)
Description

The National Drug Code (NDC) uniquely identifies drug and biological products.

Structure

Labeler – Manufacturer

Product – Strength/formulation

Package – Package size

Analytical Use

Precise drug identification

Integration with FDA and pricing datasets

Brand vs generic analysis

7. Associated_Device_or_Medical_Supply_PDI (1–5)
Description

The Product Device Identifier (PDI) for devices and medical supplies.

Use

Device-level identification

Manufacturer-specific tracking

Less standardized than NDC but critical for device analysis

8. Program_Year
Description

The reporting year in which the payment was made.

Use

Year-over-year trend analysis

Policy and market impact assessment

9. Payment
Description

The monetary value of the payment or transfer of value.

Importance

Core financial metric

Used for aggregation, trend analysis, and compliance review

10. Key Data Considerations

Multiple product columns (1–5) should be normalized (unpivoted) for analysis.

Standardization is required for categorical text fields.

Null and invalid values should be reviewed for data quality remediation.

Status: Documented
Impact: Enables accurate product-level transparency, compliance monitoring, and analytical reporting
